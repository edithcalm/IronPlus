About IronPlus

Built for
Champions

Founded in 2014, IronPlus has grown from a small garage gym into Nairobi's premier fitness destination. We believe that strength isn't just physical it's a mindset that transforms every aspect of your life.

Our state-of-the-art facility combines cutting-edge equipment with expert coaching to help you achieve results you never thought possible. Whether you're a beginner taking your first steps or an elite athlete pushing boundaries, IronPlus is your home.

Our Facilities

World-Class
Equipment

Step into a facility designed for results. Every zone is optimized for performance, equipped with industry-leading machines, and maintained to the highest standards.

Strength Zone

Premium free weights, power racks, and machines for serious lifters. Build raw power with our comprehensive strength equipment.

Cardio Area

High-end treadmills, bikes, and rowing machines with integrated entertainment. Burn calories while staying engaged.

Boxing Ring

Professional-grade boxing ring and heavy bags. Train like a champion with our combat sports facilities.

Recovery Room

Sauna, steam room, and massage chairs for optimal recovery. Rest and rejuvenate after intense sessions.

Functional Training

Open turf area with sleds, battle ropes, and plyometric equipment. Train movements, not just muscles.

Locker Rooms

Premium locker rooms with showers, towel service, and grooming stations. Arrive ready, leave refreshed.
